## 30-day validation

Measure:
- programs discovered
- programs applied
- approvals
- content published
- outbound clicks
- conversions
- revenue

Decision rules:
- No clicks: investigate traffic/content/CTA before expanding product scope.
- Clicks but no conversions: investigate product-audience fit and offer.
- Conversions: prioritize repeatable content patterns and proven programs.
- SaaS commercialization only after the internal workflow saves meaningful time and produces evidence of repeatable value.
