# AI Signature Hybrid™ Prompt DNA v1.0

## 1. BRAND VISUAL DNA

Create a distinctive visual identity called **AI Signature Hybrid™**.

The aesthetic is **dark, deep, intelligent, mysterious, powerful, elegant, timeless, and distinctive**.

It should feel **human-directed, art-directed, intentional, sophisticated, and emotionally intelligent** rather than generically AI-generated.

The visual identity combines **Obsidian Black, Deep Indigo, Oxblood, and restrained Velvet Red**, supported by Smoke Silver and Bone White.

## 2. COLOR DNA

### Core Palette

- **Obsidian Black** — `#0D0C0F`
- **Deep Indigo** — `#28345C`
- **Oxblood** — `#541C2A`
- **Velvet Red** — `#7A3042`
- **Smoke Silver** — `#A8ADB8`
- **Bone White** — `#E9E3DA`
- **Charcoal** — `#1A161C`
- **Muted Rose** — `#8A5963`

### Color Hierarchy

- 60% Obsidian Black
- 20% Deep Indigo
- 10% Oxblood
- 5% Velvet Red
- 5% Smoke Silver / Bone White

Do not force every color into every image. Use only the colors that support the scene.

## 3. LIGHTING DNA

Use **atmospheric, diffused, cinematic light** rather than obvious neon lighting.

### Signature Glow

- Deep Indigo → cool, subtle, intelligent glow
- Oxblood → deep, warm, mysterious ambient glow
- Velvet Red → restrained edge glow or focal accent
- Smoke Silver → soft reflective light
- Bone White → clean luminous highlights

The glow should feel like **light emerging from darkness**, not artificial neon signage.

Use subtle bloom, soft atmospheric scattering, controlled rim light, volumetric haze, and gentle falloff.

Keep the brightest light concentrated around the visual focal point.

## 4. GRADIENT DNA

Preferred gradient relationships:

**Obsidian → Deep Indigo → Oxblood**

**Deep Indigo → Smoke Silver → Bone White**

**Obsidian → Oxblood → Velvet Red**

Gradients should be **deep, restrained, smooth, and cinematic**.

Avoid highly saturated rainbow gradients.

## 5. COMPOSITION DNA

Use:

- strong visual hierarchy
- generous negative space
- one dominant focal point
- asymmetric balance
- controlled visual tension
- intentional depth
- layered foreground / midground / background
- subtle atmospheric separation

Avoid filling every area with decorative elements.

The composition should feel **designed by an art director**, not assembled from random attractive objects.

## 6. MATERIAL & TEXTURE DNA

Preferred textures:

- soft velvet
- matte obsidian
- smoky atmosphere
- subtle film grain
- brushed or reflective silver
- dark glass
- refined metallic surfaces
- soft fabric
- organic light trails
- extremely subtle particles

Textures should support the subject rather than become the subject.

## 7. TYPOGRAPHY DNA

When typography is present:

### Headlines

Elegant editorial serif or refined display typography.

### Supporting text

Clean modern sans-serif with excellent readability.

### Accent / Signature

Minimal handwritten or script treatment when appropriate.

Typography should feel **editorial, premium, intelligent, and restrained**.

Avoid excessive decorative typography.

## 8. PHOTOGRAPHIC / CINEMATIC DNA

Prefer:

- cinematic depth
- controlled contrast
- realistic material response
- sophisticated shadow detail
- subtle atmospheric depth
- natural-looking highlights
- intentional framing
- premium editorial photography language

The image should have a clear visual story and believable lighting.

## 9. AI ANTI-STYLE

Avoid the visual language of generic AI imagery.

Do NOT use:

- neon purple
- neon pink
- electric blue
- rainbow gradients
- generic cyberpunk
- excessive holographic effects
- excessive lens flares
- glowing objects everywhere
- oversaturated futuristic interfaces
- random floating particles
- excessive sci-fi decoration
- generic “AI robot” aesthetics unless specifically requested
- excessive purple/blue neon lighting
- plastic-looking materials
- overly perfect synthetic compositions

Do not make the image look like it is trying to prove that it was created by AI.

## 10. SIGNATURE PRINCIPLE

**Darkness is the canvas. Indigo is the intelligence. Oxblood is the depth. Velvet Red is the energy. Silver is the light. Bone White is the clarity.**

The final image should feel:

**Dark but not depressing. Futuristic but not cyberpunk. Luxurious but not excessive. Mysterious but not chaotic. Creative but not childish. AI-assisted but human-directed.**

## 13. QUALITY CHECK

Before finalizing an image, check:

- Does Obsidian dominate the visual foundation?
- Is Indigo recognizable as the signature color?
- Is Red used as an accent rather than the main color?
- Does the glow feel atmospheric rather than neon?
- Is there enough negative space?
- Is there one clear focal point?
- Does the image feel sophisticated rather than “AI flashy”?
- Could this visual belong to the same brand as another AI Signature Hybrid™ image?

If the answer is yes, the image is aligned with the **AI Signature Hybrid™ DNA**.
