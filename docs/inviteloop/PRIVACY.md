# InviteLoop Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 9, 2026

InviteLoop is a Telegram quest marketplace that lets users complete quests, earn InviteLoop Points, and use Points to create or promote campaigns.

This Privacy Policy explains what information InviteLoop processes, why it is used, and how users can contact us about their data.

## 1. Information We Process

InviteLoop may process the following information when you use the bot:

- Telegram user ID
- Telegram username, display name, and other basic profile information made available to the bot
- InviteLoop account and wallet information, including Point balances and transaction history
- Campaigns you create or interact with
- Quest reservations, completions, skips, review status, and settlement records
- Proof submitted for manual verification, including screenshots sent through Telegram
- Telegram file identifiers associated with submitted proof
- Technical and anti-abuse records needed to prevent duplicate completions, fraud, or misuse

InviteLoop does **not** require passwords, one-time passwords (OTP), private chat credentials, payment card details, seed phrases, or similar authentication secrets to complete quests.

## 2. How We Use Information

We process information to:

- Create and operate InviteLoop accounts and wallets
- Show eligible quests and campaigns
- Verify quest completion
- Prevent duplicate completions, abuse, and fraud
- Calculate and settle Point rewards
- Lock and release campaign escrow
- Maintain transaction and audit history
- Allow campaign owners or authorized InviteLoop reviewers to review submitted proof
- Investigate disputes and support requests
- Maintain the security and reliability of the service

## 3. Screenshot and Manual Proof

Some quests may require users to submit screenshot proof through Telegram.

InviteLoop may retain the Telegram file identifier and related completion metadata. Submitted proof may be shown to the relevant campaign owner or an authorized InviteLoop reviewer for verification.

Users should remove or hide unnecessary personal information before submitting screenshots. Do not submit passwords, OTP codes, payment credentials, private conversations, seed phrases, recovery codes, or other sensitive information.

## 4. Campaign Owner Review

For campaigns that use manual proof, the campaign owner may be permitted to review proof submitted for that campaign. InviteLoop limits this review to information reasonably necessary to determine whether the task was completed.

InviteLoop may also use authorized administrators as fallback reviewers, for dispute handling, or for system-owned campaigns.

## 5. Third-Party Services

InviteLoop quests may link to third-party Telegram bots, Mini Apps, channels, groups, websites, or other services.

Those services are operated independently from InviteLoop. Their own terms of service and privacy policies apply when you access them. InviteLoop does not control how third parties process information outside InviteLoop.

## 6. InviteLoop Points

InviteLoop Points are an internal platform balance used for quests and promotional campaigns. Unless InviteLoop explicitly states otherwise, Points are not cash, cryptocurrency, stored value, or a guaranteed monetary asset.

## 7. Data Sharing

InviteLoop does not sell personal data.

Information may be shared only as necessary to operate the service, including with:

- The relevant campaign owner for manual proof review
- Authorized InviteLoop administrators or reviewers
- Infrastructure and service providers used to operate InviteLoop, such as Telegram and database/hosting providers
- Authorities or other parties when required by applicable law or necessary to protect users, InviteLoop, or the public from fraud, abuse, or security threats

## 8. Data Retention

InviteLoop may retain account, campaign, completion, proof, wallet, and transaction records for as long as reasonably necessary to:

- Operate the service
- Prevent abuse and duplicate rewards
- Resolve disputes
- Maintain financial, security, and audit records
- Meet legal obligations

Some transaction and audit records may need to be retained even after an account-related deletion request so that the integrity of the Point ledger and fraud-prevention systems can be preserved.

## 9. Security

InviteLoop uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect platform data and restrict backend access. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

## 10. User Requests

Users may contact InviteLoop to ask questions about privacy or request access, correction, or deletion of account-related information where applicable.

**Telegram:** [@InviteLoopBot](https://t.me/InviteLoopBot)

Requests may require reasonable identity verification to prevent unauthorized access to another user's information.

## 11. Children's Privacy

InviteLoop is not intended to knowingly collect sensitive personal information from children. Users must comply with Telegram's terms and any age requirements applicable in their location.

## 12. Changes to This Policy

InviteLoop may update this Privacy Policy as the service evolves. Material changes may be announced through the InviteLoop bot or official InviteLoop communication channels. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when appropriate.
