# SoloForge Product Portfolio v1.0

**Status:** BASELINE PORTFOLIO MAP  
**Owner:** SoloForge AI  
**Purpose:** Define what is a brand, product, product line, service, internal component, experiment, or IP across the SoloForge ecosystem.

---

## 1. Portfolio Principle

SoloForge AI should operate as the **production studio, technology layer, and operating system behind the portfolio**.

Not everything built by SoloForge is a standalone product.

Every initiative must be classified as one of:

- **Brand** — customer-facing identity
- **Product** — something a customer can directly use or buy
- **Service** — work delivered for a client
- **Component** — reusable capability inside another product
- **Content / IP** — monetizable intellectual property
- **Experiment / R&D** — validation work, not yet a committed product
- **Internal System** — operational tooling used to run SoloForge

This distinction prevents portfolio sprawl.

# 3. Portfolio Status Definitions

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **LIVE** | Available to real users/customers |
| **SELLING** | Commercially listed or actively offered |
| **WORKING PRODUCT** | Functional product exists but commercialization may still be incomplete |
| **ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT** | Current product development priority |
| **VALIDATION** | Testing demand, workflow, or monetization |
| **CONCEPT** | Defined idea but not yet a committed build |
| **INTERNAL** | Used inside SoloForge operations |
| **FROZEN** | Retained but not an active roadmap priority |
| **ARCHIVED** | No longer worth active investment |

# 8. AI HackWork

## Channel Role

**AI HackWork = Content Lab + Experimentation Channel + Distribution Channel.**

AI HackWork is not required to own the product brand.

Its main role is to:

- test content topics
- test hooks and formats
- discover audience pain points
- validate demand
- publish practical AI / Data / Automation content
- distribute SoloForge AI technology products
- test product messaging before scaling
- feed audience and demand signals back into SoloForge

### Brand relationship

```text
SoloForge AI
→ builds / owns the product
→ packages the product
→ defines product identity
→ supplies reusable systems

AI HackWork
→ tests content
→ tests messaging
→ builds audience
→ distributes SoloForge AI products
→ collects market feedback
```

A product discovered, tested, or promoted through AI HackWork may still be sold under the **SoloForge AI** brand.

### Default rule

**Product Brand = SoloForge AI**  
**Content / Distribution Channel = AI HackWork**

Use a separate AI HackWork product brand only when there is a specific commercial reason to do so.

## 8.5 Service Work

Custom client work is optional and secondary.

Use it only when it:

1. generates useful short-term cash;
2. exposes a repeatable problem;
3. creates reusable knowledge or assets;
4. can lead to a SoloForge AI product.

The long-term goal is not to maximize service hours.

The goal is to convert market learning into scalable products.


Low fulfillment cost and globally scalable.

```text
AI HackWork Content
→ Audience / Problem Discovery
→ SoloForge AI Product
```

AI HackWork is primarily the experimentation and distribution layer.

SoloForge AI is the preferred product brand and production layer.

### D. IP / Content Revenue

```text
Books / Fiction / Digital Art
→ Catalog
→ Evergreen Discovery
→ Repeated Sales
```

Builds reusable long-tail assets.

# 16. Product Promotion Rule

Do not promote internal components as separate products unless users understand and value them independently.

For example:

**Do not sell:**
- MiniBoss Engine
- Content Engine
- Character Memory

as isolated products by default.

Instead sell the customer outcome:

- find products worth promoting
- generate ready-to-post content
- keep AI characters consistent
- automate repetitive content operations

Customers buy outcomes, not internal architecture.

---

# 17. New Product Gate

Before adding another product to the portfolio, all five questions must be answered:

1. **Who pays for it?**
2. **What recurring or painful problem does it solve?**
3. **Can existing SoloForge technology or IP build at least 50% of it?**
4. **How will it make money?**
5. **What current P0/P1 work will be delayed by building it?**

If answers are weak, classify it as **CONCEPT**, not an active product.

# 20. Final Portfolio Rule

**Build fewer products. Reuse more systems.**

The objective is not to maximize the number of products.

The objective is to turn shared SoloForge capabilities into:

```text
Reusable Technology
        +
Validated Demand
        +
Automated Distribution
        ↓
Scalable Revenue
```

Every successful service, workflow, template, content asset, and experiment should either:

- generate cash;
- strengthen the flagship platform;
- become reusable IP; or
- provide evidence for the next product decision.

If it does none of these, it should not consume active development capacity.
