## Account Separation Rules

- TECH identity owns development and infrastructure services.
- AI identity owns the primary AI workspace.
- GOOGLE identity owns Google Cloud, Gemini, and Canva.
- Do not move services between identities without an explicit migration decision.
- Never commit passwords, API keys, OAuth secrets, tokens, recovery codes, or private credentials to this repository.
