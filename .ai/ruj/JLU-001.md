# JLU-001 — Content Job List UX

Status: BUILD_COMPLETE
Mode: SELF_REVIEW until an independent context is available.
Owner approval: 2026-10-02.
Objective: Make content jobs distinguishable without memorizing short IDs, and surface jobs that need user action first.

## Scope
- Flutter content queue presentation only.
- Make Idea the primary card label.
- Move short Job ID to secondary metadata.
- Replace machine-like status text with human-readable labels.
- Group/filter jobs by Action Required, Processing, Completed, and Backlog.
- Prioritize review, publish, and failed jobs ahead of passive states.
- Show a concise next-action cue on each card.

## Exclusions
- No backend/API/state-machine changes.
- No publish behavior changes.
- No new persistence.
- No redesign of Content Job detail page.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Idea text is the primary card label; short ID is secondary.
2. READY_FOR_REVIEW, READY_TO_PUBLISH, and failure states map to Action Required.
3. GENERATING/asset/audio/render/publishing states map to Processing.
4. PUBLISHED maps to Completed; NEW/SCORED/SELECTED/BACKLOG/ARCHIVED map to Backlog.
5. Status text is human-readable.
6. Review and Publish jobs show explicit next-action cues.
7. Search remains able to find content ID, idea, status, format, and platform.
8. Existing Flutter tests plus focused queue-presentation tests pass.
