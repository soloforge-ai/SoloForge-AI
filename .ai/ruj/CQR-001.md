# CQR-001 — Content Quality Review UI

Status: DONE — merged
Mode: SELF_REVIEW. Separate agent contexts were not used; no independent QA result is claimed.
Owner approval: 2026-10-02.
Objective: Make the original idea, generated content, and semantic fidelity visible together before approval, and prevent approval when semantic evidence is explicitly failed or stale.

## Scope
- Flutter Content Job detail presentation only.
- Show Original Idea and generated Hook/Caption together before approval.
- Show semantic fidelity status, reason, required anchors, matched anchors, and generator provider/model when available.
- Block Approve when semantic fidelity reports FAIL.
- Treat semantic PASS as STALE when the draft was modified after generation; require regeneration before approval.
- Preserve approval for legacy jobs with no semantic fidelity metadata, while clearly labeling manual-review responsibility.

## Exclusions
- No backend/API/state-machine changes.
- No new persistence.
- No changes to semantic scoring rules.
- No publishing behavior changes.

## Acceptance criteria and evidence
1. PASS: Original Idea and Generated Content appear in one Content Quality Review panel.
2. PASS: PASS / FAIL / Not available / Needs recheck states are human-readable.
3. PASS: Required and matched anchors display when present.
4. PASS: Generator provider/model display when present.
5. PASS: Explicit semantic FAIL blocks Approve.
6. PASS: Fresh semantic PASS does not block Approve.
7. PASS: Missing legacy metadata remains manually reviewable and does not claim semantic PASS.
8. PASS: Malformed/missing anchor arrays are handled safely.
9. PASS: A draft updated more than two seconds after generated_at is treated as STALE and blocks approval until regeneration.
10. PASS: Flutter analyze, focused tests, existing Flutter tests, Android release APK build, and Graphify passed on final head `05f06b2b39436b45cba6a8aedfa4e603fb962c95`.
